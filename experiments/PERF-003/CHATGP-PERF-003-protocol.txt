This is the result we needed. **The CE earns its place provisionally.**

EXP-019B shows that, inside the current SYSTEM-G architecture, removing the CE drops strict Recall@10 from **41/50 to 33/50**, span recall from **0.82 to 0.66**, and MRR from **0.6009 to 0.4567**. There were **8 CE-only rescues and 0 no-CE-only rescues**; McNemar was `p=0.0078125`, and the paired bootstrap 95% CI for the strict-recall delta was **[+0.06, +0.26]**. 

The two-scale caveat still matters: this does **not** prove CE beats the best possible retrieval-only ranker. But it does answer the actual ablation question convincingly:

> **Remove the CE from SYSTEM-G as currently designed, and retrieval quality drops materially.**

So I would **not remove the CE**, and I would **not spend another experiment on shared-scale retrieval normalization right now**.
