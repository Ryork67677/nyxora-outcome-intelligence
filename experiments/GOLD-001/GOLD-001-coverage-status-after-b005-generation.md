## Projection, and what it is not

If every one of the 19 batch-005 candidates were eventually approved, the project would hold **86** eligible cases. No batch has ever approved every candidate: the four closed batches approved 16 of 18, 17 of 18, 20 of 20 and 14 of 15. Treat the number as a ceiling.

The projection must not become a reason to approve. If eighteen of these are good, the right outcome is eighteen — a hundred cases assembled by relaxing the bar measures less than sixty-seven assembled without.
