## Overview

Summary-first operational evidence with model quality presented beside business outcomes.

## Colors

Use neutral surfaces, blue for primary analytical marks, green only for favorable semantic movement,
purple for secondary comparison, and red/orange only for explicit monitoring thresholds.

## Typography

Use the system type family and compact numerical labels. Headings describe the question answered.

## Layout

The surface moves from metric strip to trend, diagnostic comparisons, and operational tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Use elevation/01 for cards and source popovers. Avoid ornamental layers.

## Shapes

Use the shared rounded card geometry and clear rectangular table structure.

## Components

The shared reader owns cards, charts, tables, source dialogs, and responsive behavior.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do preserve source provenance, consistent denominators, visible caveats, and accessible contrast.
Don't add causal headlines, mixed-scale charts, decorative effects, or hidden filters. Users can
customize manifest order, labels, and bounded datasets without changing the source contracts.
